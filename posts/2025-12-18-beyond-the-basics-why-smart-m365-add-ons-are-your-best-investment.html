Beyond the Basics: Why Smart M365 Add-Ons Are Your Best Investment

Zero Trust. Zero Drama. Zero Bullshit.

Executive Summary

For many small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), choosing the right Microsoft 365 security add-ons starts with a single, critical choice: the right plan. While the allure of a basic package like Business Standard is understandable, it often proves to be a shortsighted decision that leaves organizations exposed and inefficient. This article will explore why Microsoft 365 Business Premium is the ideal starting point for any serious cloud strategy and how a small, strategic investment in add-ons can deliver an outsized return on investment (ROI) in security, compliance, and productivity.

The Business Standard Trap: A Foundation Built on Sand

Microsoft 365 Business Standard is a popular entry point for many businesses. It includes the desktop versions of Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote (plus Access and Publisher on PC), licensed for five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five mobile devices per user, along with hosted email and Teams. Web- and mobile-only is Business Basic, not Standard — get that right before you quote it to anyone. Where Standard falls short is elsewhere: device management and security. Check current list prices on Microsoft's pricing page rather than any figure in a blog post [1]; they have moved more than once.

Standard is not empty on device management either, and it pays to know exactly where the line runs. Every Microsoft 365 for business subscription includes Basic Mobility and Security, a free MDM subset of Intune: it enrols iOS/iPadOS, Android, Samsung Knox and Windows devices, applies device policies, can remove company resources (retire), and can factory reset (full wipe) a device. So yes, you can wipe a lost phone on Standard. What you cannot do is MAM for BYOD, app deployment, macOS management, Autopilot, BitLocker key recovery, remote lock, or locating a lost device — and device compliance, Conditional Access and device configuration are all marked "limited". That is where Intune Plan 1 in Business Premium begins, and for a company with laptops and remote work, that gap is the whole game.

Similarly, the security features in Business Standard are rudimentary at best. You get basic email filtering, but you lack the advanced threat protection needed to defend against sophisticated phishing attacks, ransomware, and zero-day exploits. In essence, Business Standard leaves your digital front door unlocked and unguarded.

Management Summary

Business Standard provides the full desktop Office apps and basic MDM through Basic Mobility and Security, but lacks Intune Plan 1, Defender for Business, Entra ID P1 and Defender for Office 365 Plan 1. For organizations planning to migrate to the cloud and embrace remote work, that is insufficient as a long-term solution.

Business Premium: The Perfect Launchpad for Your Cloud Journey

This is where Microsoft 365 Business Premium shines. It is a modest step up in price from Business Standard [1], yet it closes every gap listed above. Business Premium includes everything in Business Standard, plus:

Microsoft Intune — included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium — provides mobile device management (MDM) and mobile application management (MAM) capabilities, giving you granular control over all your devices. You can enforce security policies, manage app deployments, and ensure compliance across your entire device fleet.

Microsoft Defender for Business is a comprehensive endpoint security solution that provides advanced threat protection, including endpoint detection and response (EDR). This tool helps you identify and respond to threats in real-time, protecting your organization from malware, ransomware, and other sophisticated attacks.

Microsoft Entra ID P1 is a robust identity and access management solution that enables features like Conditional Access. Wondering is Microsoft Entra ID free? The free tier covers basic identity, but P1 — included in Business Premium — unlocks Conditional Access, allowing you to enforce granular access policies based on user, device, and location, ensuring that only authorized users can access sensitive data.

Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 1 is the one people forget when they compare plans. It adds Safe Links (email, Office apps and Teams), Safe Attachments (email plus files in SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams), impersonation protection and anti-phishing thresholds — on top of the anti-spam, anti-malware and spoof protection that every cloud mailbox already has.

Microsoft Purview Information Protection is a data governance and compliance solution that helps you classify and protect your sensitive data. You can apply encryption and access controls to ensure that your most valuable information remains secure. Business Premium also includes data loss prevention for email and files — worth naming, because most comparisons quietly leave it out and it is one of the stronger arguments for the plan.

With Business Premium, you have a solid foundation for a secure and well-managed cloud environment. You can confidently embrace remote work, knowing that your data is protected and your devices are under control.

Management Summary

Business Premium provides a complete security and device management foundation for SMBs. It includes Intune for device control, Defender for endpoint protection, and Entra ID for identity management—all essential components of a modern cloud strategy.

Technical Description

The security stack in Business Premium includes endpoint protection (Defender), identity and access management (Entra ID with Conditional Access), and data protection (Purview). This combination creates a defense-in-depth approach that addresses threats at multiple levels: the network, the device, the user, and the data itself.

The Power of Add-Ons: Unleashing the Full Potential of M365

While Business Premium is an excellent starting point, it is not the end of the story. For businesses with specific security or compliance needs, Microsoft offers add-ons that build directly on it. The two that matter here are the Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium and the Microsoft Purview Suite (formerly known as Microsoft 365 E5 Compliance). Both were previously marketed as "E5 Security" and "E5 Compliance" — if you are working from an older quote, those are the same things under their old names.

The Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium: Your Digital Bodyguard

This add-on layers enterprise-grade security onto Business Premium [2]. You get:

Microsoft Entra ID P2 is the component most SMB comparisons miss, and for my money the most valuable one. It adds Microsoft Entra ID Protection — risk-based Conditional Access, the thing Business Premium's P1 cannot do — and Microsoft Entra ID Governance. If you have ever been told "just turn on risk-based policies" while sitting on Business Premium, this is the add-on that makes that sentence true.

Microsoft Defender for Identity is an identity threat detection and response (ITDR) solution that secures identity monitoring across your organization — cloud and on-premises, not on-premises only. It uses behavioral analytics to detect activity that indicates a compromised account.

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint Plan 2 adds threat hunting, live response, six months of data retention on the device, and endpoint security for IoT devices on top of what Defender for Business already gives you.

Microsoft Defender for Office 365 P2 offers advanced protection against phishing, malware, and business email compromise (BEC) attacks. Email remains one of the most common attack vectors, and this tool provides sophisticated protection against evolving threats.

Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps gives you visibility and control over your cloud apps and services. You can monitor user activities, detect anomalous behavior, and enforce data protection policies across all your cloud applications.

For a small per-user investment, this add-on provides a level of protection that was previously only available to large enterprises — and, through Entra ID P2, closes the one licensing gap that otherwise forces SMBs to jump all the way to E5.

Management Summary

The Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium extends Business Premium with Entra ID P2 (risk-based Conditional Access and identity governance), Defender for Identity, Defender for Endpoint Plan 2, Defender for Office 365 Plan 2, and Defender for Cloud Apps.

Technical Description

The add-on layers detection and response across every plane: identity risk and governance (Entra ID P2), identity threat detection (Defender for Identity), endpoint detection and response with hunting and six-month retention (Defender for Endpoint Plan 2), email threat protection with automated investigation and attack simulation (Defender for Office 365 Plan 2), and SaaS visibility and shadow IT control (Defender for Cloud Apps).

The Microsoft Purview Suite: Your No-Brainer for Regulatory Peace of Mind

For businesses in regulated industries like finance, healthcare, or law, the Microsoft Purview Suite (formerly Microsoft 365 E5 Compliance) is an absolute must-have. What it adds — and, just as important, what you already have without it:

Advanced Data Loss Prevention (DLP). Business Premium already gives you DLP for email and files. What this add-on brings on top is endpoint DLP (controlling copy-to-USB and print), DLP for Teams chat and channel messages, and the advanced classifiers. So the question is not "do I have DLP" but "do I need it where the data actually leaks".

eDiscovery (Premium). Same caveat: basic eDiscovery — search, cases, legal hold, export — is available at the E3/Standard tier. What the Suite adds is the premium set: custodian management, legal hold notifications, advanced indexing, review sets, tagging, analytics and predictive coding. Note also that Microsoft retired all classic eDiscovery experiences on 31 August 2025, including classic Content Search and classic eDiscovery (Standard and Premium) — if your runbook still points at the classic UI, it is out of date.

Compliance Manager premium assessment templates. Compliance Manager itself is broadly available; what depends on licensing is the premium assessment templates and the scope of assessments you can run against regulations like GDPR, HIPAA and SOC 2.

Records Management enables you to manage your organization's records and ensure that they are retained and disposed of in accordance with your policies. This is essential for meeting regulatory requirements and managing your data lifecycle.

If compliance is a priority for your business, the Purview Suite is a straightforward decision. It can save you a lot of manual work and help you avoid costly fines and penalties.

Management Summary

The Microsoft Purview Suite adds the advanced tier of compliance and data governance: endpoint and Teams DLP with advanced classifiers, eDiscovery (Premium), premium Compliance Manager assessment templates, and Records Management. The basic tiers of DLP, eDiscovery and Compliance Manager are already covered below the add-on.

Technical Description

The Suite implements the advanced layer of a compliance framework: data classification and protection beyond email and files (endpoint DLP, Teams DLP, advanced classifiers), premium discovery capabilities (review sets, tagging, analytics, predictive coding on top of basic legal hold and export), premium compliance assessment templates, and retention and disposal management (Records Management).

The ROI of Smart M365 Investments

It is easy to look at the cost of add-ons and see them as an unnecessary expense. However, this is a shortsighted view. The reality is that the cost of a security breach or compliance failure can be far greater than the cost of these add-ons.

Estimates for what a breach costs a small business vary widely by source and methodology — one industry estimate puts the range from roughly six figures to over a million dollars [3]. Treat any single number with suspicion; the point is only that the range starts well above what licensing costs.

So do the arithmetic yourself rather than trusting a figure from a blog post. Take the current per-user list price of Business Premium, multiply by your headcount and by twelve, then add the same calculation for the Defender Suite for Business Premium. For a 50-person company that lands in the low tens of thousands per year, a few hundred euros per employee. Compare that against your own downtime cost per day — that is the number that actually decides this, and it is the one only you have.

Investing in the right M365 plan and add-ons is not a leap of faith. It is a calculation you can run in five minutes with real prices.

Management Summary

Run the numbers with current list prices and your own headcount and downtime cost. Licensing lands in the low hundreds per employee per year; breach cost estimates start an order of magnitude higher. Do not quote a per-user price you have not checked this quarter.

Conclusion

Your journey to the cloud is too important to be built on a shaky foundation. By starting with Microsoft 365 Business Premium and adding the Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium or the Microsoft Purview Suite where your risk actually sits, you can build a secure, compliant, and productive cloud environment — grounded in a Zero Trust approach to Microsoft 365 — that will support your business for years to come.

One note on the tier above: Microsoft 365 E7 now sits over E5 and bundles the Microsoft Entra Suite and Microsoft Agent 365. For an SMB between 1 and 300 users it is rarely the answer, but if someone quotes it to you, that is what it is.

The choice is a calculation, not a leap of faith: run the numbers with current list prices and your own downtime cost. Do not let a stale price sheet decide it for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Intune included in Microsoft 365?

Microsoft Intune Plan 1 is included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium, E3, E5, and the EMS plans — but not in Business Basic or Business Standard. Basic Mobility and Security, a free MDM subset of Intune, is included in every Microsoft 365 for business subscription: it enrols devices, applies policies, and can retire or fully wipe a device. What it cannot do is MAM for BYOD, app deployment, macOS, Autopilot, remote lock, or BitLocker key recovery. If you're on Standard and need real device management, Business Premium is the upgrade that adds Intune Plan 1, Defender for Business, Defender for Office 365 Plan 1, and Entra ID P1.

Is Microsoft 365 Business Premium worth it?

For most SMBs, yes. On top of Business Standard — which already includes the desktop Office apps — it adds Intune Plan 1 (MDM and MAM), Defender for Business (endpoint protection with EDR), Defender for Office 365 Plan 1 (Safe Links, Safe Attachments, impersonation protection), DLP for email and files, and Entra ID P1 (Conditional Access). That layer is hard to match by buying the pieces separately. Check current per-user pricing on Microsoft's site rather than any figure quoted in an article.

What's the difference between the Defender Suite for Business Premium and the Purview Suite?

These are the add-ons previously marketed as E5 Security and E5 Compliance. The Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium adds Entra ID P2 (risk-based Conditional Access and identity governance), Defender for Identity, Defender for Endpoint Plan 2, Defender for Office 365 Plan 2, and Defender for Cloud Apps. The Microsoft Purview Suite adds the advanced compliance tier: endpoint and Teams DLP with advanced classifiers, eDiscovery (Premium), premium Compliance Manager assessment templates, and Records Management. Defender defends against attacks; Purview helps meet regulations like GDPR and ISO 27001.
